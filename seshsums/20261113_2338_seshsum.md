# Session summary, 2026-11-13 23:38

## What this repository is
A self-contained Lean 4 + Mathlib v4.35.0 formalization of Shrager, Hogg & Huberman (1988),
aiming at the paper "SHH Revisited: Don't Just Run Models, Reason About Them". Public at
github.com/jeffshrager/SHH88Revisited. No dependency on the CogLean lab repo; the learning-curve
predicates are copied from it with provenance. Jeff's standing instruction: push at every major
milestone without asking.

## Milestone 1 (done): the theory tier
66 theorems, no `sorry`, standard axioms only (`notes/CATALOG.md`).

- `SHH/Walk/`: the walk on 0..D as a linear system (`Sys`); the closed form `s`, proved to be its
  unique solution (`term_sys`, `sys_unique`, `sys_eq_s`); `s_one`, `s_half` (D², which fixes
  forced reflection; `stay_half_sys` gives D² + D for the other convention); `s_strictMono_D`;
  `drift_bounds` (Eq. 3, sharpened to a two-sided bound); `s_diffusion_form`; exact fan
  witnesses inside the walk; `eq2_printed` (the printed Eq. 2 equals `s`); `eq4_remainder`.
- `SHH/GraphDynamics/`: `pOf` (Eq. 1), `DOf`; the Eq. 5 omitted factor and its bound
  `exp(−D/μ)`; `curveGD` = ln n / ln(μ₀ + ct) at r = 1 (improves, strictly diminishing relative
  returns, not a power law over long ranges: `curveGD_not_powerLaw`); exact fan effects at
  n = 2¹⁶ for r = 0 (`fan_pow2_zero`: μ 2→4 raises s 256→9824) and r = ½ (`fan_pow2_half`).
- `SHH/Phenomena/`: the copied predicates plus `Worsens`; real-valued versions.

## Findings so far (status in `notes/CLAIMS.md`)
- Eqs. 2, 3, 4 are correct (2 exactly; 3 as an upper bound off by a bounded constant; 4 as the
  exponential term of 2).
- Eq. 5's approximation drops ((μ−1)/μ)^(D−1), large at small μ; with it restored, the paper's
  own idealized model shows a fan effect at r = 0, contradicting "when r = 0, increasing the
  number of edges reduces the time". The 1988 simulation at r = 0 improves, so the tree
  idealization fails there.
- Eq. 6's "power-law decay" is a logarithmic curve (under μ linear in trials): every
  qualitative power-law signature, within ~12% of a power law over trials 1–100, provably not a
  power law over long ranges.
