## The finding that links the two tiers
The tree idealization (milestone 1) predicts a fan effect at r = 0 (`SHH.GD.fan_pow2_zero`);
the bit game does not show one on average (`figure1_pattern_three`). So the paper's claim that
Eq. 2 "produces the range of behaviors observed" fails at r = 0 because of idealization I1.
